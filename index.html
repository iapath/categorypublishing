WHY I DO THIS
I claimed a category with a Mini Book... It changed everything.
I ran a training company for insurance adjusters. There was a real problem in that world and nobody had a name for it, so I wrote a small book and gave it one. About ten thousand words. It took days to write, not years.
Then the customers and industry started coming to me. People found the book, then they found me, and by the time we talked they had already decided to buy.
I thought I had gotten lucky with a book. What I had actually done was claim a category, and I didn't have the framework for that until years later.
Then I did it again on purpose. I wrote the Mini Book Model and claimed Mini Books.
Now I help solopreneurs and billion dollar brands write Mini Books to Claim their categories.
If it works in adjusting with no famous name on the cover, it isn't about the author. It's about the claim.
